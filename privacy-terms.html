Terms & Conditions and Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an InfinitySoft account, the information requested in the registration form may include your name and email address. Basic account information, such as the account creation date and account status, may also be stored to operate and maintain your account.

2. Who can see your information

The InfinitySoft site owner and authorized administrators may view your name, email address, and basic account details for account administration, support, and security. This information is not intended to be displayed publicly.

3. Password security

Your password is handled by the authentication provider and must be stored using secure password-protection methods. It should never be sent to the site owner by email or stored as readable text.

4. Why we use the information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To help provide account support and protect the service.
  • To send account-related notifications where enabled.

5. Email notifications

When the registration notification feature is configured, the site owner may receive an email when a new account is created. The notification should contain only the user's name, email address, and basic registration details—not the password.

6. Your agreement

By selecting the agreement checkbox and creating an account, you confirm that you have read this notice and understand that the site owner can view the information described above for the stated purposes.

7. Questions or privacy requests

For questions about account information or a privacy request, contact infinitysoft13@gmail.com.

Transparency note: this page explains the intended information practices. Actual account storage and email notifications will only work after a secure authentication backend and its notification service have been configured.
